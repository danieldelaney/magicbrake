Questions?

What does it do?
It takes most video formats and turns them into a small, fast, widely compatible MP4.
How does it work?
Drag into your Applications folder, open it, drop your video files onto it, click the button.
Is it really free?
Yes. Free forever, no strings attached.
Is it open source?
Yes, the source code is freely available.
What’s under the hood?
Magicbrake is powered by HandBrake, wrapped in a simple interface.
What Macs does it run on?
Requires macOS Tahoe or later. Works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
